The status quo is a zero-sum arms race

⚠ Zero-Sum Friction

  • Unilateral, nation-state AGI development incentivizes espionage, sabotage, and rushed, unsafe deployment as nations fear exclusion from the economic upside.
  • A U.S.-only nationalized program yields extreme global inequality (Gini ≈ 0.96)
  • High risk of existential conflict driven by fear of dependency
  • Safety loses every race against speed

✓ Positive-Sum Alignment

  • Decouple economic wealth from governance control, and everyone gains a vested interest in the Foundation's safe success — co-opting rivals instead of racing them.
  • Broad ownership drastically reduces expropriation risk
  • Rivals invited into the upside — removing the urgency of conflict
  • Safety becomes the profitable path

One interlocking system

Three mechanisms that only work together — legal protection, open ownership, and a binding promise to share the windfall.

◈

The Fiduciary Shield

California PBC status expands the Business Judgment Rule, legally protecting the board from shareholder lawsuits when they prioritize safety over short-term profit. It moves the litigation boundary.

◉

Open Global Investment

A broad-ownership model that invites international — even rival — participation in AGI value. Governance stays with founders via high-vote shares; upside is open to the world.

⬖

The Windfall Clause

A legal pre-commitment: profits above a set threshold are redistributed globally. It turns the fear of AGI concentration into a mechanism for shared prosperity.
